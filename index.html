Help and data How rates, KPH, and saved settings work

How to Use

Select your active XP boosts in Calculator settings. The site recalculates XP and GP rates across every tab. Your settings are saved locally in your browser.

Editable KPH

On the Slayer Tasks tab, click any KPH value to use your own kills/hour data. Custom values are saved locally and persist between sessions.

Data Sources

Kill rates are community supplied. If you have specific KPH videos that match or exceed current data, contact .saltea on Discord.

Feedback

For suggestions or issues, message .saltea on Discord.
